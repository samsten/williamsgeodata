About Us

Geo expertise,
engineering rigour.

A specialist consultancy at the intersection of geographic information science and Python software engineering.

GeoCore Solutions was founded on a simple conviction: that the real value in geodata lies not in the raw files, but in what you do with them. Too often, organisations collect rich spatial datasets that sit underused — too complex to analyse, too unwieldy to share.

We bridge that gap. With a background spanning remote sensing, GIS analysis, and production Python development, we take projects from raw data to deployed insight. Whether it's building an automated processing pipeline, designing a spatial database schema, or creating an interactive map your stakeholders will actually use — we handle the full stack.

We work with clients in environmental consulting, infrastructure, local government, maritime industries, and research. Every engagement is collaborative, clearly documented, and built for longevity — not just a one-off deliverable.
