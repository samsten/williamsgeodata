About Us

Geodata expertise,
finding new ground.

A specialist consultancy on the edge of geographic information science and Python software engineering.

Williams Geodata was founded on a simple conviction: that the real value in geodata is not in the data itself, but how it is used. Too often, organisations collect large amounts of spatial data without much ability to upscale their systems.

This is where we come in. With a background spanning GIS analysis, remote sensing, geodesy, surveying, data science and Python development, we take your processing power to the next level. Whether it's building an automated processing pipeline, designing a specialised application, or researching new ways to improve your data management.

We work with clients in environmental consulting, infrastructure, local government, maritime industries, and research. Every engagement is innovative, collaborative, clearly documented, and built for longevity.
